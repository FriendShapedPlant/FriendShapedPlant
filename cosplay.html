Cosplays

I've been cosplaying on-and-off since 2017, I won't ever share pictures publicly but I made a Mangle costume from Five Nights at Freddy's for Halloween and it all went down from there! Nowadays I tend to go for somewhat niche characters and play fast and loose with the materials and presentation.

My cosplay pages serve as both a showcase and an outline of their build processes. Very verbose pages ahead!

Still a work in progress, you can find most of my cosplays on this Notion page in the meantime.

Costubers

The Costubers, or Cosplay Youtubers, I follow! I'm ALWAYS looking for more, please suggest me some!

Lizard Leigh
Number one top fave costuber. Combines his craft with video essays and stories either related to his costume or to the activity of cosplay, including conventions. I revisit many of his videos all the time.
Sarah Spaceman (Surplus)
This is her second channel, which I think I may prefer the video style of more? Sarah livestreams giving you updates on whatever she is currently working on! She really goes into detail on the steps, stitches, tools, and where she got them. Her main channel also covers such things in more of a video essay format, as well as her experience at conventions and other tangentially geeky otaku sewing stuff.
Colleen Cosplay
Old school style videos featuring cosplay AMVs and vlogs of her costume building process and her time at conventions! She's also a wheelchair user and discusses disability in relation to her cosplays. Has a few tutorials as well!
Anya Panda
Classic cosplay storytimes, costume walkthroughs, con vlogs, and other fun cosplay-related videos! She does a lot of stuff on a budget too.
Lee the Leef
Video essayist cosplayer and idol who discusses their experiences at cons, especially as a disabled person, as well as the cosplay community as a whole. Also posts about general costume-y and nerdy stuff!
