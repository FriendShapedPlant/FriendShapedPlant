Banette

Created May 2026

The 354th pokémon, introduced in generation three, and my favorite Pokémon despite not playing the games. Actually I don't engage with Pokémon much at all, but this guy still holds a special place in my heart.

This shrine is about my relationship with Banette, for a shrine-esque page about the Pokémon, check them out on Jamie's Friend-dex!

A Wild Banette Appears!

Back in my DeviantArt days, I participated in TommyGK's Charity Guild Collabs as much as I could. They were 99% Pokémon themed, and I would use a Random Pokémon Picker site to shuffle through until I found a neat one to draw! This was how I first met Banette.

Banette was the last of three Pokémon I drew for the 2017 Pokémon Orchestra Collab, I was on a roll that year. I'm pretty sure the randomizer just chose standard Banette, but in researching I found their Mega evolution, which I of course preferred; it's got more going on visually, which makes it interesting, so thats what I drew! Ironically they almost look like the shiny version with how light and blue that grey marker looks in the scan, twas not intentional. I still think this drawing holds up well despite the passage of time!

I Choose You!

I was a Creepypasta kid, so of course I watched videos along the lines of, "Top 10 DARKEST Pokémon that will KILL YOU!!!" and, "Top 50 SCARIEST Pokémon Origins!" I was very familiar with the Driftloons, Yamasks, Bewears, Phantumps, and Froslasses of the Pokéworld. I don't remember Banette being included in such a video, but they definitely fit the vibe with their vengeance and cursing.

I've also got that classic Plush Empathy Autism. I have a huge attachment to this large dog plush I got at 2 or 3 years old who still sleeps with me in my bed 20+ years later. My sibling does not have such care for plushies and it would make me so upset thinking about them being abandoned under their bed for years. I feel really bad when my plushies fall off the bed or are left in a physically uncomfortable position for a person to sit in to this day! Needless to say I feel for Banette. I think I could fix them.

Also for any fellow Plush Empathizers who also worry when their plushies fall off, its okay! They love a little floor time, they don't mind!

Bag

I don't have much Banette merch in my personal possession, but heres the cards I own and want!

This was my first Banette card! I was at a convention, passed a booth setting up to sell some Pokémon cards, and I muttered something about finding Banette. The vendor searched their stash, and sure enough they had just that one! And they gave it to me free of charge it was so sweet 🥺 It's a neat little design, contrasting their creepiness against other children's toys.

I got this card in a very similar way to the previous card, people are so nice!!! It depicts a really interesting scenario. Why is Banette in the woods? I always thought them to be more urban, maybe suburban. Looks really neat though!

I bought this and the next card for a dollar. I REALLY like the art of this one. It gives me the impression of them being left in the attic with all these other forgotten toys and old furnishings, and they've made the space their own. Another great contrast like the first card as well!

I bought this and the previous card for a dollar. Honestly this one looks really weird to me, the open zipper and stance and angle look awkward. Would not have got if it weren't for the previous card but I have it now lmao.

I would love to have this card. Seeing them in such a girly bedroom with makeup and accessories in front of them is so cute. It also makes me ask, is this their trainers room? Banette's room? Are they a pampered princess? Did they break into this house for the sillies? Who's to say?

I just learned about this card, the colors on it are so good! It's a lot newer though so I don't think I'll get it in my hands anytime soon rip. If the illustration without card text ever surfaces please let me know!!!

Outside of cards, I don't yearn much for Banette merch. I got enough characters to amass plastic things of, but I have considered a simple sticker or two.

This Mega Banette crop top fucking owns though? Oh my shit? I can live without it but if I ever find it for a reasonable price I am eating it up. I love a crop top and the zippered deets are an awesome touch. Would be even cooler if they installed actual plastic zippers in the shirt, maybe not functional ones but y'know. Or maybe I don't know fashion well and it would suck bad.

I do dream to one day have a custom-made Banette plush. They are literally a toy/plush/doll pokemon it would be so awesome. The official ones are so small and stiff, and I know they canonically got stiff arms but I think for a plush they should be limp and floppy and the tail on their head should also be floppy and their head overall should rest at a tilt. A life-sized plush sounds cool but the fact they're 3 feet tall kinda scares me so I think I'll go with 13 inches tall, give or take. This is a far off goal though, not something I'll obtain for years to come. If you know any poképlush makers though, I'd love suggestions for when I do get this fella made!
