Biker

Origin Hotline Miami (Game)
Year Finished 2024
Status Casual

Intro

Hotline Miami isn't my kinda gameplay, but I am obsessed with its themes and aesthetics. My cosplay homie has a Jacket cosplay, and I thought it would be cool to do Biker with him! Luckily all the pieces perfectly sort of fell into place to make this easy as hell to execute. Tis a very comfy cosplay minus the fact I cannot see through that visor.

Photos

Helmet

The bike helmet used to belong to my father, and was older than me so probably past its prime protecting condition. I had to remove the stickers he put on it, sand its glossy finish, then prime and spray paint cyan.

The visor was initially clear so I purchased Rust-Oleum Lens Tint and sprayed the inside. Unfortunately, I was not as careful as I should have been as I put on too much and cannot see through it very well! It's all dark and cloudy. Looks great from the outside.

I had issues with the visor tint sticking to the rubber lining the face opening. I shittily covered the rubber lining with some glued-on black fabric and colored in the chipped off tint with sharpie. Sticks less and you can hardly tell theres any tint missing!

Vest

The puffer vest was purchased at Target on sale! They don't sell it anymore obviously but the label is Wondershop.

The skull decals are made with Siser heat transfer vinyl. I hand-cut the designs based around how Tatsu draws the skull, and very carefully ironed them on. The garment care instructions say to not iron the vest At All so I was a little scared! I got the decals most of the way adhered, and anything that didn't stick I used fabric glue for.

Other

The gloves and weapon were both purchased at Spirit Halloween. Fingerless cyan gloves are impossible to find and I don't have it in me to try and make a pair! Skeleton gloves fit the vibe close enough.

Phone prop was found thrifting, it's a Sony Cordless Telephone SPP-75. When not being used to receive hits it sits on my wall with my other old phones looking awesome.

The rest of the fit is super duper easy, I've had white sleeveless shirts forever and any old pair of jeans will do.
